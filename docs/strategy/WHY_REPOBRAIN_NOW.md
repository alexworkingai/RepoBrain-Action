# Why RepoBrain Now

## Context

AI coding tools are increasingly capable, but team adoption still depends on trust, governance, and operational clarity.

## Current Gap

Teams often lack a bounded control layer that clearly answers:

- what is supported right now,
- what was blocked and why,
- what can be trusted as evidence,
- where to stop and escalate instead of improvising.

## RepoBrain Relevance

RepoBrain is timely because it prioritizes:

1. governed runtime behavior over implicit autonomy claims,
2. explicit supported/unsupported boundaries,
3. artifact-backed operational truth,
4. protected-kernel-safe external product framing.

## Strategic Value

At startup stage, this creates a practical evaluation profile:

- externally legible,
- pre-marketplace but usable,
- bounded and auditable,
- suitable for design-partner validation without over-claiming maturity.

## Boundary Reminder

RepoBrain today is strongest as a governed orchestration/control shell around accepted surfaces, not as a breadth-first feature platform.
